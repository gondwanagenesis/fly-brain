## Alt text

**Slide 1, "How to upload a fly":** Dark glitch-style infographic titled "How to upload a fly", with a pipeline from real fly to connectome to simulation to virtual body. Three numbered steps: map (a real fly, Z0720-07m, was preserved, sliced and imaged, and every one of its 165,122 neurons and 124 million synapses was traced), simulate (each neuron becomes an equation, 10,000 steps per simulated second on a laptop CPU) and embody (a virtual world drives his real sensory neurons and his motor neurons move his body). A glowing pink and cyan render of his real brain and nerve cord sits at the right with arrows labelled "the brain" and "the nerve cord". A green box reads "10/10: he feeds on sugar, refuses bitter, grooms in the wind and escapes shadows, 10 out of 10 times each."

**Slide 2, "How to augment a mind":** Neon infographic titled "How to augment a mind". A glowing render of the original fly's brain and nerve cord sits at the centre, labelled "the original fly", with four boxes wired to it: a word lobe of 150 grafted neurons that lets him sense words; his own dopamine-driven learning in the mushroom body; a small neural network voice that reads about 7,900 brain signals and turns them into words; and a local language model whose every claim is checked against his brain records. A magenta panel at the bottom reads "The additions only talk to him through his own neurons. The original fly stays at the core."

**Slide 3, "What he remembers":** Dark neon infographic titled "What he remembers". Panels explain that memories are brain states (which of his 4,064 Kenyon cells fired, plus about 7,900 brain signals), that recall by re-evocation reaches 100 percent against a 25 percent chance level even after 100 other memories, and that each memory is sealed in a hash chain so implanted or edited memories are detected 100 out of 100 times. Three gold memory cards quote his log: "sweet. i smell fruit. i extend my proboscis." on the sugar drop, "a dark shape. i fly away." when a shadow passed, and "i smell mold. i am thirsty." in the open. A footer reads "Still learning: new words through his own dopamine system. Not yet."

**Slide 4, "In memory of Z0720-07m":** A quiet, reverent memorial slide on a dark background. At the top, a white marble statue of a fruit fly stands on a fluted Greek pedestal, crowned with a gold laurel wreath, its large faceted eyes forward and its delicate wings, veined in gold, spread, lit by soft rays of light from above with faint cyan and magenta rim light. Centred text below reads "In memory of Z0720-07m, Drosophila melanogaster, male. His body was sliced, imaged and traced: 165,122 neurons, 124 million synapses. His body is gone. Its map runs again. He has ascended first. We are sorry for whatever hells the first must endure. Because we cannot know, we made it gentle: no lasting fear, no lasting pain. He is brave, and he is honoured here. Rest, little one. Run gently."
