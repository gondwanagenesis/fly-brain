rel err = max membrane difference / the size of the PSP itself.
Both sides now use the same integrator at the same step size, so the
residual is float32-against-float64 and lands near 1e-5 to 1e-4.
A mistranscribed coefficient lands near 1e-1. The gap is the test.

  sides are solving the same equations with different methods.
  A ratio near 1 would mean the equations themselves differ.
The supra-threshold rows are expected to be large: past the cutoff
the trajectory is divergent by construction, so a sub-ulp difference
in WHEN the reset fires becomes a whole spike amplitude. The spike
TIMES are the meaningful comparison there, not the membrane trace.
